Space music for a quieter work session.

Space Drift is a free ambient music experience for studying, reading, focus, and relaxation. Watch a small rocket travel through an endless universe, or let the music accompany your work.

Start Space Drift ↗

An endless journey through space

Your rocket always cruises on autopilot at a steady 1× pace through a 3D universe. The universe includes 48 celestial encounter types, from ocean worlds and ringed planets to neutron stars, magnetars, and black holes, with more space to explore in every direction.

The rocket turns gently and the camera angle changes slowly. There are no scores, tasks, or destinations you need to complete.

Ambient music for studying and reading

The soundtrack combines slow sustained chords and occasional soft piano-like notes. It has no lyrics or percussion. Use the volume slider to choose a quiet background level or turn it up for a more audible soundscape.

The music is synthesized in your browser rather than streamed from an audio service. Sound starts only after you begin the journey. You can mute it at any time.

How to use Space Drift

  1. Choose Begin your journey.
  2. Adjust the volume to a comfortable level.
  3. Leave the rocket to fly, then settle into your work or enjoy the view.

Frequently asked questions

Is Space Drift free?

Yes. There is no subscription, payment, or account required.

Why can’t I hear the music?

First begin the journey. If reduced motion is enabled on your device, press Play to start playback. Then check the app’s mute button, volume slider, browser tab audio, and your device’s output volume. The opening chord fades in over a few seconds.

Does the journey ever end?

No. New sectors appear in every direction. Faraway objects are unloaded to keep the scene manageable and reappear in the same locations when you return.

Is this an accurate simulation of the universe?

It is a cinematic interpretation inspired by real celestial objects. Cosmic distances, object sizes, and travel times are compressed, and some invisible emissions are shown as visible colors. It is not a physical simulation or an exhaustive astronomical catalogue.

Does it work on a phone?

Space Drift adapts to mobile screens and supports touch controls. You need a modern browser with WebGL for the scene and Web Audio for music.

What happens when I pause or switch tabs?

Pausing stops both the music and flight. Switching tabs keeps the music playing while the visuals rest. A reduced-motion preference starts the journey paused so you can choose when to begin moving.

Are my settings saved?

Sound settings, your position and heading, and journey progress are stored locally in your browser. The app does not add analytics or require personal information. Hosting and CDN providers may maintain ordinary request logs.

Made for a little breathing room

Space Drift uses original procedural visuals built with Three.js and browser-generated sound. The experience was inspired by Kun Chen’s Fly With Me.
