Space Drift is a quiet companion for working, reading, or exploring. Cruise on autopilot, or take control and fly in any direction through an endless 3D universe. Turn around to revisit the worlds behind you.

Release steering to keep flying on your new heading. Set speed to zero to look around in place. Autopilot resumes from your current location; it never teleports you back. Its camera changes gently every few minutes. Solid worlds have a surface boundary so you can fly around them.

Inspired by real astronomy. Distances, sizes, and travel times are compressed; high-energy emissions are interpreted as visible light. Sound is a cabin ambience. This is a cinematic journey, not a physical simulation.

Created in your browser. No accounts or tracking. Inspired by Kun Chen’s Fly With Me.
