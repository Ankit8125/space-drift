An hour-long opening route, followed by endless new passages. The camera gently changes its view every few minutes. Dragging gives you the view back.

Inspired by real astronomy. Distances, sizes, and travel times are compressed; high-energy emissions are interpreted as visible light. Sound is a cabin ambience. This is a cinematic journey, not a physical simulation.

Created in your browser. No accounts or tracking. Inspired by Kun Chen’s Fly With Me.
